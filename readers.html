Build the missing picture

Ask readers how they describe themselves.

A future opt-in survey should separate gender identity, age band, region, preferred format, heat tolerance, genre promise and discovery path. Include “another identity”, “prefer not to say” and “unknown”. Never infer gender from a name, photograph, purchase or reading history.

Publish sample size, recruitment method, field dates and missing responses beside every chart.
