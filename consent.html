Imagine / Consent Compass

The edge only works when everyone can step back.

Consent is informed, freely given, specific, ongoing and reversible. AI, chemistry, popularity and group pressure do not get to replace a human yes.

Original generative artwork · 2026

Editorial principle

People are people, not content, proof, tokens or project resources.

All fictional intimate participants in this public project are adults. Consent is informed, freely given, specific, reversible and ongoing. A relationship, marriage, household, contract or prior yes does not create unlimited permission.

Informed

The person knows what is being asked

Identity, purpose, risk, audience, data use and likely consequences are not hidden behind romance or technical language.

Freely given

No penalty is attached to no

Housing, work, status, immigration, care, money, community access or AI support are not held hostage to agreement.

Specific

One yes does not unlock everything

Affection, intimacy, recording, publication, data sharing, business and travel each require their own clear agreement.

Ongoing

Consent is checked in the present

A character can pause, renegotiate or stop. Silence, chemistry and algorithmic confidence are not substitutes.

Reversible

Withdrawal changes what happens next

A genuine exit is practical, safe and free from retaliation. Previously agreed uses are reviewed where possible.

Capacity and power

Context can make a yes unreliable

Age, coercion, intoxication, dependency, authority, deception, synthetic influence and unequal resources need explicit narrative consequences.

AI boundary

A machine may assist. It may not declare consent.

An AI could support translation, scheduling, reminders, scenario testing or private reflection. It cannot read a body signal and convert that signal into permission. It cannot decide that collective benefit outweighs one person's refusal.

Allowed story role

Help adults compare options, remember boundaries and request a fresh human conversation.

Unsafe shortcut

Scoring desire, inferring attraction, predicting a yes or treating biometrics as clinical proof of consent.

Review question

Can the least powerful person understand, override and leave the system without losing access to ordinary life?

Public-site red lines

The host has a boundary too.

This GitHub Pages site stays non-explicit and editorial. It contains no sexualised minors, graphic sexual acts, exploitative imagery or onsite sales checkout. Book interiors, covers, previews and retailer listings still need separate review.

GitHub policy is not a substitute for law

Hosting policy can be narrower than national law and can change. See the GitHub sexually obscene content policy and GitHub Pages limits.

Edition review

Ask what the story rewards.

A narrative may depict poor choices, conflict or harm without endorsing them. The review question is whether power and consequence remain legible, whether the adult genre promise is honest and whether marketing strips away the context that made a difficult scene defensible.
