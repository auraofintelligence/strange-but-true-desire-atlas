The author mission

Build a long catalogue and the world around it.

The Desire Atlas supports Luke's work across speculative near-future fiction, science-fiction romance and romantasy. It brings the practical questions into one public place: who may read the books, where those people gather, which events matter, what market evidence says, how many sales a goal implies, which channels carry each format and what changes for each country edition.

Global group marriage, emerging intelligence, Love United Nations and joyful responsible abundance are part of the worldview driving the books. This atlas connects those ideas to audience, community, publishing and country reach.
