# Hero image generation record

Five project-bound raster images were created with the built-in image generation tool on 15 August 2026. The original PNG outputs remain in Codex's generated-image folder. The public site uses compressed WebP copies in `assets/images/`.

Every prompt required clearly adult, fully clothed people; non-explicit scenes; no celebrity likenesses; no brand marks; no readable text; no logos; and no watermark.

## `hero-home.webp`

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: full-width website hero background for Strange But True: The Desire Atlas. Create a cinematic near-future world of love, literature, travel and human-AI possibility from a high coastal observatory at blue hour. Show a curved Earth horizon, luminous cities and ocean, and subtle orbital pathways forming an abstract heart-shaped constellation. Place a diverse ensemble of clearly adult explorers and writers, approximately ages 30 to 60, as independent equals around an open circular table with one unoccupied chair. Books, a small star map and soft holographic threads suggest many possible stories. Use premium cinematic science-fiction concept art with realistic human warmth. Keep subjects on the right half and generous dark negative space on the left for large white copy. Use magenta, coral and golden light against midnight indigo. The mood is hopeful, intimate, daring and responsibly abundant. Do not depict sexual activity, a wedding ceremony, ownership symbolism, a robot lover or humanoid AI.

## `hero-tribe.webp`

Create a dazzling near-future international gathering of romance, romantasy and science-fiction readers, writers and independent publishers at an open-air book festival woven through a futuristic coastal city. Add aurora-like ribbons and a subtle heart constellation. Show diverse adults aged roughly 25 to 70 meeting in small respectful circles, swapping books, listening and laughing. One quiet welcoming circle has an empty chair. Use cinematic editorial concept art with sunrise coral, electric magenta, violet and deep blue. Keep people mainly on the left and centre, with clean darker space on the right. No giant book covers.

## `hero-boundaries.webp`

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: full-width hero for consent, legal boundaries and responsible publishing pages. Show explorers pausing at a luminous threshold at the edge of a near-future city. The setting is a transparent civic observatory where glowing paths divide towards open, caution and closed horizons, with ocean and stars beyond. Three clearly adult writers stand independently with books and small compass lights, consulting one another before crossing. Transparent doors and open exits symbolise ongoing consent and legal clarity. Use premium architectural science-fiction realism with indigo, amber, coral and restrained red. Keep the subjects and threshold on the left, with deep dark negative space on the right. Do not use police, prisons, weapons, courtroom symbols or warning text.

## `hero-storyworld.webp`

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: full-width hero for a speculative storyworld about plural futures, chosen family and emerging AI. Show a hopeful near-future household and creative cooperative in a spacious biophilic home-workshop overlooking ocean and stars. Separate private rooms visibly branch from a shared central commons. A diverse group of clearly adult people aged 30 to 60 gather by choice around a generous shared table, each with an individual light and distinct personal space. Ambient AI appears only as soft non-humanoid patterns in the architecture. Include one open doorway and one empty chair. Use warm gold, coral, plum and midnight blue to convey joyful responsible abundance, dignity, autonomy and possibility. Do not include wedding iconography, collars, ownership or hierarchy symbols, or a robot person.
