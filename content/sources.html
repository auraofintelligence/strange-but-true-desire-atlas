Method

Source, scope, date, gap.

The Atlas prefers legislation, regulators, official platform policies, official event pages and named industry measurement. A source can still be old, narrow or incomplete, so the visible claim is never stronger than the source.

1 · Source

Who said it?

Link to the government, regulator, organiser, retailer or named research body rather than a search result or recycled summary.

2 · Scope

What did it actually cover?

Keep year, geography, format, sample and definition beside the number or legal statement.

3 · Date

When was it checked?

Events and platform rules can move quickly. Law translations and old operational guidance may trail the current authoritative text.

4 · Gap

What remains unknown?

Write DATA NOT YET VERIFIED or TO BE CONFIRMED instead of completing the picture with a guess.

5 · Decision

What can this evidence support?

A market signal may support a small test. Legal guidance may support a question for counsel. Neither automatically clears a release.

6 · Boundary

Should it be public?

Personal and culturally controlled material stays private even when it could make the story or dataset more interesting.

Known gaps

The missing pieces are part of the map.

The launch register is intentionally incomplete. It covers a first jurisdiction and platform set well enough to frame expert questions, not enough to operate a global automated clearance engine.

Comparable audience data

Current romance-reader gender, age, format and discovery data is not comparable across the target countries.

Country depth

Scotland, Northern Ireland, individual US states, Australian states and territories, and Gulf states beyond the UAE need their own records.

Tax, consumer and privacy law

The first release focuses on content and distribution boundaries. Sales tax, refunds, privacy, email marketing and business registration need a separate workstream.

How old notes were used

Private market, travel and group-marriage notes supplied the questions and story themes. They were not treated as factual authority, copied wholesale or published with personal details. Every public legal, platform, event and market claim was re-sourced.
