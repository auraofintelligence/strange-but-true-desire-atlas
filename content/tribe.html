Community before conversion

Friends, not data points.

A tribe is not a list of accounts to harvest. It is a place where people already have norms, histories and reasons for being together. The useful question is not “How do I sell to them?” but “What can I learn, offer and make clearer?”

Arrival protocol

Listen, contribute, ask permission.

Every room has a different boundary around self-promotion, research, direct messages and sensitive topics. Read the current rules before posting. If a group is for readers, do not turn it into free market research. If a group is private, keep what you learn there private.

1 · Observe

Learn the actual conversation

Notice what people celebrate, criticise and protect before deciding your work belongs in the room.

2 · Participate

Offer useful attention

Recommend others, answer in good faith and bring curiosity that would still matter if you had nothing to sell.

3 · Ask

Permission comes before research

Use approved threads and moderator guidance. Never scrape profiles, infer identities or move a conversation off-platform without a mutual yes.
