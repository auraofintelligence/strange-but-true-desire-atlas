Choose the room

Reader joy, writer craft and rights business are different journeys.

A signing event can reveal reader energy. A craft conference can sharpen the work. A rights fair can open translation and distribution conversations. One expensive trip rarely does all three equally well.

Reader-facing

Look for access, signing format, expected audience, genre fit and promotion rules. Plan a welcome, not a hard sell.

Writer-facing

Choose sessions that answer a current craft or business question. Leave room for peer conversations that are not transactional.

Trade and rights

Prepare a rights sheet, edition status and clear questions. Confirm whether an appointment, accreditation or professional pass is required.

Travel privacy boundary

The public Atlas records event place and official dates. It does not publish personal routes, bookings, accommodation, daily movements, private meetings or attraction notes. Keep those in a private travel plan.
