The worldview behind the catalogue

Love, intelligence and civilisation are all allowed to evolve.

These future worlds are where personal belief, long-range goals, current questions and fiction deliberately cross-pollinate. They are not one prediction or one programme. They are different windows into how love, family, business, intelligence and global cooperation might grow.

Love as infrastructure

Affection and care can shape homes, organisations, economies and institutions rather than existing only inside private couples.

Emerging intelligence

Artificial general intelligence may become translator, collaborator, coordinator, cultural bridge, business participant or a new kind of social presence.

Plural family and enterprise

Global group marriage explores love, family and business extending into larger adult constellations built for connection and shared abundance.

Protopian institutions

Democracy, diplomacy and public leadership can be imagined as systems that mature through each generation rather than claiming perfection.

Future-world shelf

Six possibilities behind the books.

Each premise can stand alone or touch the others. Some remain close to the near future; others reach much further into social, technological and spiritual possibility.

Future-world premise

Love United Nations

A future variation of the actual United Nations in which representatives are emotionally and spiritually evolved people who form genuine loving bonds across borders. The people asked to unite the world live the unity they represent, moving diplomacy beyond national ego, partial democracy, dynasty and authoritarian power.

Future-world premise

Global Group Marriage

Adults across cultures extend love, family and business into a larger shared life, creating new possibilities for companionship, care, travel, enterprise and joyful responsible abundance.

Future-world premise

The Translation Moon

An ambient intelligence translates language, culture and context while each person continues to express their own meaning, desire and choice.

Future-world premise

House of Many Doors

A chosen family designs a generous home whose shared spaces, private spaces, work, rest and movement can expand as the household changes.

Future-world premise

The Abundance Audit

A civilisation promising abundance learns to count care, time, land, energy, attention, beauty and shared purpose alongside money.

Future-world premise

The Unregistered Marriage

A plural family builds love and enterprise across several countries while the legal systems around them still recognise only fragments of the whole.

Joyful Responsible Abundance

All three words carry the future.

Joy keeps the worlds alive with delight, play and beauty. Responsibility gives connection the depth to last. Abundance opens the possibility that love, intelligence, opportunity and belonging can grow together.

Joyful

Desire, humour, wonder, discovery and celebration remain part of the architecture rather than rewards postponed until everything is solved.

Responsible

People can understand one another, make clear choices, revise agreements and remain full participants in the lives they help create.

Abundant

More connection becomes possible as relationships, knowledge, care, technology and shared enterprise create value together.

From vision to audience

Let the future worlds inform the atlas.
