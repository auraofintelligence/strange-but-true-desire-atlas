Two starting architectures

Exclusive ebook or wide experiment?

Both are proposals to test, not universal formulas. A catalogue can also change strategy after an enrolment term or edition cycle, provided current contracts and reader promises are honoured.

Exclusive ebook test

KDP Select ebook test

Use a defined enrolment period to learn from Kindle Unlimited and Amazon sales while keeping print available through other routes. Measure read-through, pages read and opportunity cost.

Wide ebook test

Wide ebook distribution

Publish direct where practical and use an aggregator for additional retailers. Track whether reach offsets added metadata, pricing and account complexity.

Reader-direct route

Direct reader relationship

Build an email list and reader home, then connect them to a suitable commerce and delivery service when direct sales become part of the plan.

What current public channel data can show

Readers use several routes at once.

BookNet's 2025 study of English-speaking Canadian buyers reported 53% of their purchases online and 47% in person; 76% were print, 14% ebook and 7% audiobook. Those are purchase and format shares within the surveyed buyer sample, not retailer market shares.

Among past-year audiobook listeners in the Audio Publishers Association's February 2026 US consumer study, overlapping access included 49% buying through an app or website, 48% using a subscription, 46% using a digital-library app and 42% using service credits.

Comparable platform sales volumes are not public

KDP, Kobo, Apple, Google, Draft2Digital and Ingram do not publish one current, directly comparable public table of independent-author unit sales by platform and country. The Atlas therefore shows current terms and broad channel behaviour, then leaves actual catalogue units, pages read, returns and receipts to Luke's own private dashboards.
